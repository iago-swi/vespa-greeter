========================================================
  VESPA GREETER - PASSO DEL SALUTO (Windows Offline Edition)
========================================================

HOW TO PLAY ON WINDOWS:
1. Keep the "Vespa Greeter" folder structure intact (index.html, css/, js/, vendor/).
2. Double-click "Play Vespa Greeter.bat" (or double-click "index.html").
   Your default browser (Edge, Chrome, or Firefox) will launch the 3D game.
   Runs 100% offline — Three.js is bundled locally inside vendor/three.min.js.

FEATURES:
- Vespa Garage: Pick Vintage Mint, Rosso Corsa, Cream White, or Alpine Blue in real time.
- Time of Day: Toggle Golden Hour, Crisp Alpine Morning, or Twilight (with working headlights & warm tunnel lighting).
- Alpine Pass Scenery: Kilometer markers, Passo del Saluto summit arch, wooden chalets, stone mountain refuges, road dust, and sun lens flare.
- Local High Scores: Separate Top-5 leaderboards saved automatically for both 2-Lap Scenic Tour and Endless Cruise modes.

CONTROLS:
- SPACE BAR : Salute oncoming motorbike riders
- W / S or Up / Down : Accelerate / decelerate (with 2-stroke exhaust pops!)
- A / D or Left / Right : Gentle lane nudge
- C : Toggle camera (Chase View / First-Person Handlebars View)
- H : Classic Vespa horn beep
- T : Cycle Time of Day / Atmosphere
- M : Mute / Unmute sound
- ESC : Finish & save score (Endless Cruise) or return to Garage

RULES:
- Salute oncoming motorbikes that raise their left hand BEFORE they pass (+100 to +150 pts, plus streak multipliers).
- Do NOT salute riders who keep both hands on the bars, or cars, trucks, and cyclists (-50 pts).

Enjoy Passo del Saluto!
